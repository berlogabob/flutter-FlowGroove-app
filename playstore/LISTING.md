## Full description

FlowGroove keeps your band on the same page — literally.

**Your songs, one library.** Keys, BPM, tags, lyrics and chords (ChordPro). Search-powered
autofill fetches artist, BPM and lyrics when you add a song. Import/export CSV.

**Setlists that don't lie.** Build setlists for gigs and rehearsals, reorder by drag,
export to PDF (full, compact, or a printable pack). Song counts always match what's inside.

**Practice tools built in.** Metronome with tempo ramp, count-in, subdivisions and haptics —
it keeps clicking with the screen off. Chromatic tuner with on-device pitch detection
(nothing is recorded or uploaded). Performance sheet shows chords over lyrics on stage,
with transpose.

**Made for bands.** Create a band, invite bandmates with a QR code or link, share songs and
setlists, plan rehearsals and vote on times. Everyone sees the same, current version.

Free, no ads. Built by a musician, one devlog at a time — flowgroove.app/blog
