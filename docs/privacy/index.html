This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use FlowGroove.

What Data We Collect#

Information You Provide#

  • Account data: Email address, display name
  • Content data: Setlists, songs, notes, and other content you create
  • Support data: Bug reports, feature requests, emails

Information We Collect Automatically#

  • Usage data: How you use FlowGroove (via Google Analytics 4, anonymized)
  • Device data: Device type, browser type, operating system
  • Log data: IP address, access times, pages viewed

Information We Don’t Collect#

  • We don’t collect precise location data
  • We don’t collect biometric data
  • We don’t collect financial information (no payment processing on our side)
  • We don’t sell your data to third parties

How We Use Your Data#

We use your data to:

✅ Provide the service: Sync your setlists across devices
✅ Improve the product: Understand which features are most used
✅ Communicate with you: Respond to support requests, send updates
✅ Ensure security: Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
✅ Comply with legal obligations: Meet EU regulatory requirements

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)#

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your data based on:

  1. Contractual necessity — To provide FlowGroove services you requested
  2. Legitimate interests — To improve and secure our product
  3. Consent — For analytics and marketing cookies (you can withdraw anytime)
  4. Legal obligation — To comply with EU laws and regulations

Who We Share Your Data With#

We don’t sell your personal data. We only share with:

Service Providers#

  • Google Firebase: Cloud storage and real-time sync (encrypted)
  • Google Analytics 4: Anonymous usage analytics
  • Microsoft Clarity: User behavior heatmaps (anonymized)

Legal Requirements#

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and property.

How We Protect Your Data#

  • Encryption in transit: All data sent via HTTPS/TLS
  • Encryption at rest: Data stored encrypted in Firebase
  • Access controls: Only authorized personnel can access data
  • Regular audits: Security reviews conducted quarterly
  • Backups: Daily automated backups with 30-day retention

Cookies#

FlowGroove uses the following cookies:

Essential Cookies#

  • Session cookie: Keeps you logged in (required)
  • Preference cookie: Remembers your theme choice (required)

Analytics Cookies#

  • GA4 cookie: Anonymous usage statistics (optional)
  • Clarity cookie: User behavior tracking (optional)

You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings or via our cookie consent banner.

Data Retention#

We retain your data for as long as:

  • Active account: Indefinitely
  • Deleted account: 30 days (backup retention), then permanently deleted
  • Inactive account (12+ months): We’ll email you before deletion

Children’s Privacy#

FlowGroove is not intended for children under 16. We don’t knowingly collect data from children under 16. If we learn we have, we delete it immediately.

International Data Transfers#

Your data may be processed outside the EU (via Google Firebase in the US). We ensure:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with processors
  • Adequacy decisions where applicable
  • Equivalent protections for all transfers

Changes to This Policy#

We may update this policy. We’ll notify you of material changes via:

  • Email to registered users
  • Banner on the website
  • Updated “Last Updated” date
