# Home

Your dashboard. From here you can jump to your songs, bands, setlists, and tools.

- Tap a navigation tab at the bottom to switch sections.
- **Menu (⋯)** in the bottom bar opens the current screen's actions and your
  [Profile](../profile/).
- **Practice** (Quick Actions) is a preview — the practice workbook
  (exercises, progress tracking, homework) is coming soon.
- Quick tips and recent activity show on this screen.
