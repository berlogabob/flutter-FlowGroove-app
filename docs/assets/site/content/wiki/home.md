# Home

Your dashboard. From here you can jump to your songs, bands, setlists, and tools.

- Tap a navigation tab at the bottom to switch sections.
- **Menu (⋯)** in the bottom bar opens the current screen's actions and your
  [Profile](../profile/).
- The **practice dashboard** on top shows this week's minutes, streak and a
  7-day graph — tap it for the full [Practice](../practice/) screen.
- **Quick Actions**: create a Song, Band or Setlist — or **Audio note**, which
  starts recording the moment it opens (link it to a song later from the
  Recorder).
- **Tools**: Tuner, Metronome, Rehearsals, **Recorder** (all your unlinked
  takes in one place) and **Practice** on the full-width bottom row.
