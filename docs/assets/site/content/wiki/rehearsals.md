# Rehearsals

Agree on when to rehearse — and tie it to the music.

Open a band and tap **Rehearsals** to plan the next session.

## Propose a time

- An editor or admin creates a rehearsal proposal with a title and one or more
  **time options**.
- Mark members as **Required** or **Optional** (tap a member to cycle
  Required → Optional → off).
- Optionally attach a **setlist**, a **location**, and notes.

## Vote

- Every member answers **Can**, **Maybe**, or **Can't** for each time option.
- FlowGroove highlights the **best slot** — it never picks a time where a
  required member can't make it, and prefers times with the most people
  available. You still confirm the final choice yourself.
- The proposal shows who hasn't voted yet.

## Confirm and share

- The organizer confirms one time; the rehearsal becomes a confirmed event with
  the attached setlist and everyone who said they're coming.
- **Add to calendar** exports an `.ics` file for Google Calendar, Apple
  Calendar, or Outlook.
- Members who linked Telegram get a heads-up when a proposal is created and when
  it's confirmed.
