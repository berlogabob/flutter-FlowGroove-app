How to easily and quickly add Sections to your practice
Our solution is driven to streamline your practice, save time, and improve your experience. It not only allows you to loop song parts with utmost precision but also lets you rename each section for increased clarity.
1. Choose your favorite song and upload it
Upload any song from your library or any public URL. Multiple audio and video formats are supported.
2. Click on the 'sections' button next to the play
Within seconds, our app will identify and display each song's sections. Each beginning section will precisely start at the right downbeat.
3. Tap on each section button to begin looping
When you tap into a section, it will automatically create a looping. You can tap in more than one section to loop multiple parts.
4. You can rename your sections as desired
Each section has a default set of names, for example, Sections A, B, and C. But, we offer easy two-tap editing with a list of sections to choose from, such as Intro, Chorus, Verse, and more.
