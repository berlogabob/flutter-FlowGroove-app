### Existing shared clock and mutual exclusion

- The shared `BackingEngine` is created once by `backingEngineProvider` and exposed through the singleton `backingTransportProvider` ([backing_transport_provider.dart:26–42](</Users/berloga/Documents/GitHub/flutter_repsync_app/lib/providers/data/backing_transport_provider.dart:26>), [backing_transport_provider.dart:648–651](</Users/berloga/Documents/GitHub/flutter_repsync_app/lib/providers/data/backing_transport_provider.dart:648>)). On web, it uses the metronome audio context and bar timing ([backing_transport_provider.dart:28–37](</Users/berloga/Documents/GitHub/flutter_repsync_app/lib/providers/data/backing_transport_provider.dart:28>)).
- Drum practice routes its pattern loading, launch, and stop through that same backing transport ([drum_practice_provider.dart:89–103](</Users/berloga/Documents/GitHub/flutter_repsync_app/lib/providers/data/drum_practice_provider.dart:89>), [drum_practice_provider.dart:106–120](</Users/berloga/Documents/GitHub/flutter_repsync_app/lib/providers/data/drum_practice_provider.dart:106>)). Its step clock tracks drum step position; the backing engine handles playback ([drum_practice_provider.dart:66–80](</Users/berloga/Documents/GitHub/flutter_repsync_app/lib/providers/data/drum_practice_provider.dart:66>)).
- The transport serializes engine work and clears the engine when rendering begins ([backing_transport_provider.dart:161–173](</Users/berloga/Documents/GitHub/flutter_repsync_app/lib/providers/data/backing_transport_provider.dart:161>)). Attaching a song clears the practice pattern ([backing_transport_provider.dart:235–237](</Users/berloga/Documents/GitHub/flutter_repsync_app/lib/providers/data/backing_transport_provider.dart:235>)); attaching a pattern begins a new render on that same engine ([backing_transport_provider.dart:182–187](</Users/berloga/Documents/GitHub/flutter_repsync_app/lib/providers/data/backing_transport_provider.dart:182>)). Metronome stop also stops the transport ([backing_transport_provider.dart:131–138](</Users/berloga/Documents/GitHub/flutter_repsync_app/lib/providers/data/backing_transport_provider.dart:131>)).

Selecting an instrument view should be presentation-only. Attaching a new transport clears or reloads the shared engine, resets playback state, and can interrupt the active song or drums. The providers show that attachment is a playback operation, not a view-selection operation.

### Regression acceptance checks

1. **Drums:** Opening or selecting the drums view during playback preserves the current shared transport and position; explicitly loading a pattern attaches it through the shared transport.
2. **Keys:** Selecting keys during song playback leaves the song’s transport, metronome clock, and active position unchanged.
3. **Guitar:** Selecting guitar during playback does not clear or reattach the shared engine; existing playback continues.
4. **Song workspace:** Switching into or between song workspace views preserves the current song transport; only an explicit song attach/load changes its backing.
