The Story Behind FlowGroove

Hi, I’m a solo developer who’s played in cover bands for 10 years.

Every rehearsal was the same: paper setlists, lost BPM notes, someone forgetting the key changes. The chaos was killing our vibe.

So I built FlowGroove.

It’s the tool I wished we had. Simple. Fast. Works offline. Syncs in real-time across all our phones.

No venture capital. No corporate team. Just a developer who plays in a band and got tired of the chaos.

Now you can use it too. Free for indie musicians.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is FlowGroove really free?
Yes. Core features are free forever for indie musicians. I built this to solve my own band’s problems. If it helps yours too, that’s a win. I accept donations on Ko-fi to keep servers running and coffee flowing.
Who's behind this?
Just me — a solo developer who plays in cover bands. My company, Sounding Doubts (based in Portugal), handles the legal side. But the code, design, and vision? All one person.
Does it work offline?
Yes. FlowGroove caches your setlists locally. No internet at the venue? No problem. Changes sync when you’re back online. Perfect for basement rehears and outdoor gigs.
How fast is the sync?
Under 500ms. Change a song on your phone, it updates on the band’s tablets before you finish strumming the first chord. Tested on 3G, WiFi, and everything in between.
Can I use it for my professional band?
Absolutely. FlowGroove works for any band — indie, semi-pro, or full-time. The free tier covers most use cases. Power users can support development on Ko-fi for advanced features.
