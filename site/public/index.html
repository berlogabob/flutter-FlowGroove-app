The story

Built by a musician,
for musicians

Solo developer
10 years in cover bands

Every rehearsal was the same: paper setlists, lost BPM notes, someone forgetting the key changes. The chaos was killing our vibe.

So I built FlowGroove — the tool I wished we had. Simple, fast, works offline, syncs in real-time across all our phones. No venture capital. No corporate team. Just a developer who plays in a band and got tired of the chaos.

Now you can use it too. Free for indie musicians.
