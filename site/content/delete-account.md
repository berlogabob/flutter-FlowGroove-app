# Delete Your Account

You can delete your FlowGroove account, and everything tied to it, at any time.

## Delete it in the app

1. Open FlowGroove and sign in.
2. Go to your **Profile**.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account** (just below *Sign out*).
4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

## What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

- your account and sign-in,
- your personal **song library**,
- your **setlists**,
- your **practice history** (metronome sessions, weekly minutes, streak, logbook),
- your **band memberships**,
- your profile data (display name, photo).

Bands you share with other members are not deleted — only your membership is
removed. A band that you were the **only** member of is removed with your account.

This action is **permanent and cannot be undone**.

## Can't sign in?

If you've lost access to your account and can't delete it from the app, email
**[privacy@flowgroove.app](mailto:privacy@flowgroove.app)** from the address tied
to your account and we'll handle the deletion for you. We respond within 30 days.

## Data retention

After deletion, residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within **30 days**.
See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for full details.
