  {{< section-title >}}The Story Behind FlowGroove{{< /section-title >}}
  {{< story-content >}}
Hi, I'm a solo developer who's played in cover bands for 10 years.

Every rehearsal was the same: paper setlists, lost BPM notes, someone forgetting the key changes. The chaos was killing our vibe.

So I built FlowGroove.

It's the tool I wished we had. Simple. Fast. Works offline. Syncs in real-time across all our phones.

No venture capital. No corporate team. Just a developer who plays in a band and got tired of the chaos.

Now you can use it too. **Free for indie musicians.**
