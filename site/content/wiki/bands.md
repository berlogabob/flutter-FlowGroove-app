# Bands

Organize rehearsals with your group.

- Create a band, then invite members with a join link.
- Membership is managed on the server — joins go through a secure flow.
- Each band has its own songs, setlists, and about page.
- Band setlists export and share just like personal ones: the card's **⋯ menu**
  offers *Copy links* and *Export PDF* (**As is** / **Compact**), and the
  metronome button queues the whole setlist.
- Plan the next session with [Rehearsals](../rehearsals/) — an availability poll
  that turns into a confirmed rehearsal with a setlist.
