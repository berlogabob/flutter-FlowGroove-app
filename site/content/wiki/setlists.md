# Setlists

Order songs for a performance.

- Create a setlist and add songs from your library or a band.
- Tapping a setlist opens a **read-only view** first — editing is behind the
  explicit **Edit** action (in the Menu sheet), so nothing gets reordered by
  accident.
- Reorder songs to match your show.
- If a song was deleted from the library it still shows in the setlist as
  **"Unavailable song"** — the count stays honest and you decide when to
  remove the entry (swipe it away; **Undo** appears if you change your mind).
- Deleting a setlist can also be undone from the snackbar for a few seconds.
- Open a setlist in Concert Mode to perform.
- From a setlist card's **⋯ menu**: share the songs as links, or export the setlist
  as **PDF** — **As is** (one detailed card per song) or **Compact** (one line per
  song, fits a single list).
- The **Open in metronome** button queues the whole setlist in the metronome.
