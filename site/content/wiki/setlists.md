# Setlists

Order songs for a performance.

- Create a setlist and add songs from your library or a band.
- Tapping a setlist opens a **read-only view** first — editing is behind the
  explicit **Edit** action (in the Menu sheet), so nothing gets reordered by
  accident.
- In the editor, **long-press a song and drag** to reorder it.
- If a song was deleted from the library it still shows in the setlist as
  **"Unavailable song"** — the count stays honest and you decide when to
  remove the entry (swipe it away; **Undo** appears if you change your mind).
- Deleting a setlist can also be undone from the snackbar for a few seconds.
- Open a setlist in Concert Mode to perform.
- Each setlist card shows **one pinned quick-action button** plus a **⋯ menu**.
  Choose what the pinned button does in **Settings → Quick action on setlist
  cards** (default **Open in metronome**, which queues the whole setlist).
- The **⋯ menu** has: **Edit setlist**, **Event kit**, **Share** (songs as
  links), **Copy links**, and **Export PDF** — **As is** (one detailed card per
  song) or **Compact** (one line per song, fits a single list).

## Event Kit — the show, not just the songs

Open **Event kit** from a setlist card's **⋯ menu**; a small 🎭 badge on the card
shows once a kit is set up. For setlists tied to a real event:

- **Stage plot** — a simple stage editor: place members, instruments and gear
  on a stage grid.
- **Role cards** — who plays what, per song; changes for a song (capo,
  instrument swap, backing track) live with the setlist.
- **Event guide PDF** — the setlist export sheet has a *With event guide*
  toggle: one document with the setlist plus the stage plot and role pages —
  print it, tape it backstage.

## Sharing an event with one person

Give a sound engineer, a venue or a guest musician access to **one event**
without adding them to the band. Works for band setlists and personal setlists.

1. Open the setlist's **⋯ menu → Event kit** and set the date and venue.
2. Under **People**, add the person (name and role). A role preset ticks the
   areas they get: **Event info**, **Running order**, **Song detail**, **Stage**,
   **Rider**, each *hidden*, *view* or *edit*. Adjust the ticks if needed.
3. Tap the **share** icon next to their name and send them the link.
4. They open it: no sign-in is needed to read. To edit their area they sign in
   and tap **Claim**; the event then appears in their setlists list.

They only ever see the areas you gave them. Remove the person or lower their
access and it takes effect right away. Links expire after a while and can be
revoked. Guests with *suggest* rights can propose changes; you accept or
decline them in the event's inbox.
