title: "The Tuesday the setlist got lost (again)"

We lost the setlist. Again. Third rehearsal in a row.

Somebody's phone had the "real" version, except it didn't, because the real
version was on a different phone at 4% battery in a bag in the other room. We stood
around for fifteen minutes — guitars on, ready to play — arguing about whether the
second set opened with the fast one or the slow one.

I build software for a living. I'd spent that same week shipping features to
strangers on the internet, and here I was, unable to answer "what song is next?"
for my own band.

That was the night it clicked: this is a solvable problem, and I'm exactly the
person who should solve it. Not because I'm special — because I was the one
standing there annoyed enough to do it.

The next posts are where I actually start building. This one's just the part where
I got fed up.
