Cover bands are not a wealthy people. I've been in enough of them to know the budget
is "whatever's left after strings and the rehearsal room." An app that costs money
to organize a hobby is an app nobody installs.

So FlowGroove is free for indie musicians, and that's a deliberate product choice,
not a missing business model. "Free" doesn't mean "worthless" — it means the value
shows up somewhere other than a paywall: in a band that actually adopts it because
there's no card to enter, no per-seat math, no trial countdown.

The support side is honest too. There's a Ko-fi link for anyone who wants to keep
the servers running, and that's it. People will back a solo project when you're
straight with them about what it is and what it costs *you* to run.

I'd rather have a thousand bands using something free than ten paying for something
they resent. Free is the feature that gets it onto the music stand.
