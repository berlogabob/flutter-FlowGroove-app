## What happened

As I write this, **FlowGroove is #1 on TinyLaunch** — 39 upvotes, ahead of a couple of
sharp marketing and AI tools. A metronome-and-setlist app for cover bands, at the top of a
launch board. I did not have that on the card.

Thank you. Genuinely. Every one of those votes is a person who read one line — *keep your
band in sync: setlists, tempo, concert mode* — and decided that was worth a click. That's
not nothing.

## The honest part

A leaderboard is a vanity number. It measures a good day, not a good product. The number I
actually watch is smaller and quieter: a band opening a setlist five minutes before they go
on, the metronome not drifting, the sync holding on bad venue wifi. That's the win that
doesn't show up on a launch board.

So I'm keeping this in proportion — grateful for the day, back to the work tomorrow. If the
line above sounds like your band, [try it](https://flowgroove.app). It's free, and the demo
account is real: three bands, real songs, real setlists, no sign-up.

To everyone who voted, and to the bands already using it on stage — thank you. This is a
better week because of you.
