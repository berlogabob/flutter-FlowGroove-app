# 5 Problems Every Cover Band Faces (And How I Fixed Them)

I've played in cover bands for 10 years.

Every band I've been in faced the same 5 problems. Every. Single. One.

Here they are — and how I fixed them with FlowGroove.

---

## Problem #1: The Lost Setlist

**The scenario:** You arrive at rehearsal. The setlist was on a piece of paper. Someone spilled coffee on it. Or lost it. Or "forgot to bring it."

**The cost:** 15 minutes of "What were we playing again?" before you even start.

**The fix:** 

FlowGroove stores your setlists digitally. They're synced across all devices. No paper. No coffee stains. No excuses.

Add songs in 3 seconds. Share with the band. Done.

---

## Problem #2: The BPM Argument

**The scenario:** "Is 'Wonderwall' 87 BPM or 89?" "Does it matter?" YES. It matters when the drummer starts at 95 and the whole song falls apart.

**The cost:** 5 minutes of tapping tempo, checking YouTube, arguing. Momentum: gone.

**The fix:**

FlowGroove auto-detects BPM. Type the song name → get the exact BPM. No arguments. No tapping. No YouTube searches mid-rehearsal.

If the API is down (rare), you can enter it manually. No blocking errors, ever.

---

## Problem #3: The Key Change Confusion

**The scenario:** "What key is this in?" "I thought it was G." "No, it's definitely F#m." *Plays chord* "Yeah, that doesn't sound right."

**The cost:** 10 minutes of transposition, Capo math, and frustration.

**The fix:**

FlowGroove auto-detects the musical key. Same flow: Type the song → get the key. The guitarist can calculate Capo positions. The keyboardist can prep chords. Everyone's on the same page.

---

## Problem #4: The Device Disconnect

**The scenario:** You updated the setlist on your phone. The guitarist has it on his tablet. The drummer printed it. Nobody has the same version.

**The cost:** Confusion, mistakes, and wasted rehearsal time.

**The fix:**

FlowGroove syncs in real-time. Change a song on your phone → it updates on all devices in <500ms. Everyone sees the same setlist. Always.

Tested on 3G, WiFi, and spotty venue internet. Works everywhere.

---

## Problem #5: The Dark Gig

**The scenario:** You're on stage. It's dark. You pull out your phone to check the setlist. The screen brightness blinds the bass player.

**The cost:** Awkward moments, squinting, and looking like amateurs.

**The fix:**

FlowGroove has a stage-ready dark mode. High-contrast, easy to read, won't blind your bandmates. Designed for dim venues and late-night gigs.

---

## The Common Thread

All 5 problems have one thing in common: **They're solvable with better tools.**

Paper setlists → Digital setlists  
BPM arguments → Auto-detection  
Key confusion → Key detection  
Device disconnect → Real-time sync  
Bright screens → Dark mode  

That's why I built FlowGroove. Not because I could. Because I had to.
