# 5 Problems Every Cover Band Faces (And How I Fixed Them)

Ten years of cover bands, and somehow every single one of them ran into the same five problems. So I'll walk you through them — and how FlowGroove deals with each.

---

## Problem #1: The lost setlist

You show up to rehearsal and the setlist — the one piece of paper — is gone. Coffee got on it, or it's at home, or someone "forgot." Now you're spending the first fifteen minutes going "wait, what were we playing?"

FlowGroove keeps your setlists digital and synced across every device. No paper, no coffee casualties, no excuses. Add songs in a few seconds, share with the band, done.

---

## Problem #2: The BPM argument

"Is 'Wonderwall' 87 or 89?" "Does it matter?" It matters when the drummer counts in at 95 and the whole thing wobbles off a cliff. Then it's five minutes of tapping tempo and checking YouTube while the energy drains out of the room.

FlowGroove pulls the BPM for you — type the song name and there it is. If the lookup is ever down, you can enter it by hand. Nothing blocks you.

---

## Problem #3: The key-change confusion

"What key is this?" "I thought G." "No, F#m." *plays a chord* "...yeah, that's not it." Ten minutes of transposition and capo math later, you've lost the thread.

FlowGroove detects the key the same way: type the song, get the key. The guitarist works out the capo, the keys player preps the chords, and everyone's finally on the same page.

---

## Problem #4: The device disconnect

You updated the setlist on your phone. The guitarist has last week's version on his tablet. The drummer printed it out in 2023. Three people, three different setlists, zero agreement.

FlowGroove syncs in real time. Change a song on your phone and it updates on everyone's screen almost immediately — even on patchy venue internet. One setlist, everywhere.

---

## Problem #5: The dark gig

You're on stage, it's dim, you pull out your phone to check what's next, and the screen lights up the whole front row — right into the bass player's eyes.

FlowGroove has a proper stage-ready dark mode: high-contrast, easy to read, and it won't blind your bandmates. Built for dark venues and late nights.

---

## The common thread

Every one of these comes down to the same thing: the right tool just didn't exist for us.

- Paper setlists → digital ones
- BPM arguments → automatic detection
- Key confusion → key detection
- Mismatched devices → real-time sync
- Blinding screens → dark mode

That's the whole reason FlowGroove exists. Not because I could build it — because I was tired of not having it.

## What FlowGroove does

It's a free, real-time setlist sync app for cover bands. It handles all five problems above, plus a couple you haven't hit yet:

- Auto BPM & key detection for any song
- Real-time setlist sync across every device
- Offline mode for signal-free rehearsal rooms
- Stage-ready dark mode for gigs
- Free for indie musicians
