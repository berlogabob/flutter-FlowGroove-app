## What changed

A dedicated performing view: the app, reduced to what matters when you're actually on
stage.

- **Wakelock:** the screen stays on. Nothing worse than your setlist sleeping between
  songs while you fumble to wake it with a pick in your hand.
- **Big touch targets:** controls you can hit without looking, because your eyes are on
  the room, not the glass.
- The same real-time sync underneath, so the band moves through the set together — when
  one person advances, everyone's on the same song.

## Why it matters

Managing songs and *performing* them are two different modes with opposite needs. At a
desk you want density and options; on stage you want one big obvious thing and nothing
else. Concert Mode exists because the most important moment for a band app is the gig
itself, and a gig is the worst possible time to go hunting through menus. Design for the
hands-full, low-light, high-stakes moment, and the rest of the app can stay calm.
