For months FlowGroove has been a quiet project — built between rehearsals, tested
at my own gigs. Three shows now, run entirely on the app: songs, setlists, the
metronome, all of it on stage with me instead of a folder of coffee-stained paper.

Along the way I fixed a long list of small annoyances and a few genuinely scary
bugs. Somewhere in there it stopped feeling like a prototype and started feeling
like something I trust in front of an audience.

So it's time to say the word out loud. I'm starting a TinyLaunch campaign for
FlowGroove — not to chase numbers, but to find the people who'd actually use it:
bands drowning in the same scattered-chat, lost-setlist, "what's the BPM again?"
chaos I built this to fix. I want testers, honest feedback, and a few more
musicians I can help untangle their band's mess.

If that's you — or you know a band like that — cheer me and FlowGroove on. This is
the part where it stops being just mine.
