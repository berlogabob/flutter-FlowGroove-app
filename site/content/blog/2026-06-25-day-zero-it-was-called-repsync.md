## What changed

On 2026-02-03 the whole thing was one commit: "Initial commit." A blank Flutter
project, a README, and a working name — **repsync**, as in "rehearsal sync."

- The name was a placeholder that almost stuck, the way placeholders do.
- A few weeks in, the rename landed: repsync became **FlowGroove**, and a later pass
  scrubbed the last "RepSync" references out of the web build so nothing leaked the
  old identity.
- Nothing about day 0 was impressive. It was a scaffold and an intention. That's
  what day 0 always is.

## Why it matters

It's easy to look at an app with sync, a metronome, autocomplete, and bands and
assume it arrived fully formed. It didn't — it started as an empty project with a
clunky name, and every feature since is a commit on top of that first one. If you're
staring at your own blank `flutter create`, that's not behind. That's exactly where
this started too.
