# Questions People Actually Ask Me

## The basics

### Is FlowGroove really free?

Yes — the core stuff is free, and it's staying that way for indie musicians.

I built this to fix my own band's mess. If it fixes yours too, great. There's a Ko-fi if you want to chip in for servers and coffee, but you never have to.

Here's what the free tier covers:

- Unlimited setlists
- Real-time sync across your devices
- Auto BPM & key detection
- Offline mode
- Dark theme

### Who's behind this?

Just me. One developer who plays in cover bands.

The code, the design, the questionable color choices — all one person. My company, Sounding Doubts (Portugal), handles the legal side so I don't have to think about it.

Ten years in cover bands means I know the pain points firsthand. I lived every one of them.

### Does it work offline?

Yes. Your setlists are cached right on your device.

No signal at the venue? Doesn't matter — you can still read and edit everything. When you're back online, it syncs up on its own. No lost changes.

## Features

### How fast is the sync?

Fast enough that you won't notice the wait.

Change a song on your phone and it lands on the band's tablets about as quick as you can look up. It holds up fine on flaky venue WiFi and phone data too.

### Can I use it for my professional band?

Of course. Indie, semi-pro, full-time — it doesn't care. The free tier covers most bands; if you want to support the extras, that's what Ko-fi is for.

### What devices does it work on?

- Web browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)
- Android phones & tablets
- iOS (coming — I'm sorting out the Developer License)
- Basically anything with a modern browser

### How does auto BPM & key detection work?

Type a song name and it pulls the BPM and key for you.

It looks the data up from music databases. If that's ever unavailable, you can just type the numbers in yourself — nothing ever blocks you.

## Privacy & legal

### What data do you collect?

Only what the app needs to work:

- Account info (email, name)
- Your setlists and songs
- Anonymous usage analytics (via GA4)

I don't sell data and I don't follow you around the web. The full [privacy policy is here](/privacy/).

### Is my data safe?

Your setlists live on Firebase (Google's cloud). That means:

- Encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
- Encrypted at rest
- Daily backups
- And one developer who actually notices when something breaks
