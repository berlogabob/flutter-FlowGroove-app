## Copywriting Principles (ENFORCED)

### Tone & Voice:
- **Authentic:** Solo dev, not corporation
- **Conversational:** Like talking to a bandmate
- **Passionate:** Music-first, tech-second
- **Transparent:** Open about journey, struggles, wins
- **Encouraging:** "You can do this too" energy

### Writing Rules:
- Short sentences (≤20 words)
- Active voice (90%+)
- Specific numbers (not "many", use "10 years")
- Benefit-first headlines
- Feature-second descriptions
- Always include CTA

### Conversion Framework (AIDA):
- **Attention:** Hook with relatable pain
- **Interest:** Show the solution
- **Desire:** Paint the transformation
- **Action:** Clear, single CTA

## Content Templates

### Hero Section:
```markdown
# Headline (H1)
[Benefit-driven statement, ≤10 words]

## Subheadline (H2)
[How it works + who it's for, ≤20 words]

### Primary CTA
[Action-oriented, ≤3 words]

### Secondary CTA
[Lower commitment, ≤5 words]
```

### Feature Description:
```markdown
### [Feature Name]
**Benefit:** [What user gets, not what feature does]

[1-2 sentences explaining how it works]

[Specific metric or proof point]
```

### FAQ Item:
```markdown
**Q: [Question in user's words]?**

A: [Direct answer in first sentence]

[2-3 sentences of context/examples]

[Reassuring close or CTA]
```

## Voice & Tone Guide

### Voice Characteristics:
| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Authentic | Real person, real story | "I built this between rehearsals" |

| Encouraging | "You can do this" | "Try it, it's free" |

### Vocabulary:
**Use:**
- "I" (solo dev)
- "Your band" (not "users")
- "Gig", "rehearsal", "setlist" (musician terms)
- "Free", "simple", "fast"
- "Built", "made", "crafted"

**Avoid:**
- "We" (not a team)
- "Users", "customers", "clients"
- "Leverage", "synergize", "optimize"
- "Enterprise", "scalable", "robust"
- Corporate buzzwords
